What to copy: (1) Lead with a claim or verdict (“Is the Best…”, “Isn’t…”, “You’re Sleeping On It”), not a topic. (2) Put a person or emotion first (“The Guilt of…”, “Trauma With Tenderness”) rather than the game’s name. (3) Kill the fillers — “A Quick Look”, “One Year Later”, “Demos”, poetic “Zen & The Art Of”, and stuffed #hashtags all read as low-value. (4) Length isn’t the lever here (winners and losers both average ~75 chars) — the presence of a stated argument is.
