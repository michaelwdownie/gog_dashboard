3 · Title patterns that correlate with more views

The single clearest signal on the channel: winners lead with a provocative thesis or emotional hook; the game is named second. Losers name the game first and bury it under "|| A Video Essay" markers and hashtag strings.

What's working

  • 467,328v · 3465/day Killing Nazis Isn't Woke — The History and Politics of the Wolfenstein Trilogy
  • 3,930v · 101/day The Corporation Is the Monster - Alien: Isolation's Real Horror
  • 35,811v · 83/day The Best Game I Ever Hated - The Last of Us Part II Critique
  • 33,254v · 71/day The Guilt of James Sunderland || A Silent Hill 2 Remake Retrospective #SilentHill2 #Essay #lore
  • 2,732v · 25/day Metal Eden Is a Banger and You're Sleeping On It - Intermission 07
  • 1,048v · 14/day The Dad Game That Replaced Trauma With Tenderness

What's not

  • 224v · 0.4/day The Games I Loved In 2024 || #videoessay #goty #goty2024 #videogames #gameawards
  • 520v · 0.8/day Zen & The Art Of Deep Space Mining || EVE Online || A Video Essay #eveonline
  • 294v · 0.7/day From Sci-Fi to Horror: Three Isolated Demos || Intermission 01
  1. Lead with a claim, not a game name. "Killing Nazis Isn't Woke", "The Best Game I Ever Hated", "The Corporation Is the Monster" — every top performer front-loads an argument or feeling. The game becomes the subtitle after a dash.
  2. Delete "|| A Video Essay", "|| Intermission 0X" and hashtag strings from titles. Zero top performers use them; nearly every underperformer does. They add no search value and consume the ~55 characters a viewer actually sees on mobile. Move hashtags to the description.
  3. Be contrarian or emotionally loaded. "Isn't Woke", "Brave Enough to Not Be", "You're Sleeping On It", "The Game I Ever Hated" — tension and stance out-perform neutral description.
  4. Keep the searchable keyword visible. The game/franchise name is the SEO anchor — pair the hook with it ("… — A Silent Hill 2 Remake Retrospective"), don't drop it, but don't lead with it either.
