3 · Title patterns that correlate with better performance

Derived from this channel’s own 18 long-form essays, ranked by views-per-day. The viral outlier is included as a pattern, not a fluke — its hook is the same shape as the top essays.

  • Front-load an emotional or contrarian hook (first ~40 characters).
    Winners lead with tension: Killing Nazis Isn’t Woke (498,562), The Best Game I Ever Hated (36,294), The Guilt of James Sunderland (34,747), The Corporation Is the Monster (4,925 in 59 days). Flat praise underperforms: …Is the Best Game FromSoftware Ever Made (1,354).
  • Name the franchise — but after the hook, via a dash or subtitle.
    Every top essay leads with a concept and names the game second (… — The Last of Us Part II Critique). Videos that hide the game name entirely (The Dad Game… = Pragmata) or bury it behind pipes (… || EVE Online) starve.
  • Kill title-stuffed hashtags and “|| Intermission ##” / “|| A Video Essay” suffixes.
    No top performer carries them; several low performers do — The Games I Loved In 2024 || #videoessay #goty #goty2024… = 228 views. They burn the ~50 visible mobile characters and shove the game name off-screen. (Keep real tags — the API shows top essays already have 13–24 backend tags; those belong in the tags field, not the title.)
  • Keep the visible front half tight (≈30–60 chars).
    The longest title on the channel — A Quick Look At Wuchang… | First Impressions & Gameplay (81 chars) — earned 677 views. Winners run 29–63 chars up front.

Underperformers

  • One subject, big and high-contrast.
    The winning thumbnails read as a single dominant character/subject against a clean background (James Sunderland; the TLOU2 face). The strongest fix for the underperformers is the same: one hero subject, nothing competing with it.
  • The franchise name legible at mobile width (~210px).
    If “SILENT HILL f”, “PRAGMATA”, “CYBERPUNK 2077” or “ARMORED CORE VI” can’t be read as a thumbnail-sized image, the video loses the search-and-suggest browsers the rewritten titles are meant to attract. Aim for ≤3 words of text at large size.
  • Consistency as a brand signal.
    Give the recurring franchises (Silent Hill, and future essays) a shared template — same font family, same text placement, same color treatment — so a viewer who watched the 34k SH2 essay instantly recognises the SH f one as “more of that.”

Method & caveats. This analysis uses public metrics only via the YouTube Data API v3 — view / like / comment counts, titles, tags, durations, publish dates and thumbnails. It has no access to CTR, impressions, audience retention or traffic sources (those require a YouTube Analytics OAuth connection). Every “this drives views” claim here is therefore an informed hypothesis from public signals, not a measured cause. “Views-per-day since publish” is used to compare videos of different ages fairly; like-rate (likes ÷ views) is the proxy for “content quality” behind the discovery-problem framing.
